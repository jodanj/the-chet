The Chet

The Chet is a small, free community website It is a hobby project and is not affiliated with any other company or service.

What we collect

  • Accounts need only a username and password. No email address, phone number or real name is asked for.
  • Passwords are never stored. Only a salted PBKDF2 hash is kept.
  • We never ask for payment details, and nobody running this site will ask for your password in chat.
  • Guests can join without an account and nothing about them is stored in the account database.

Deleting your account

Open the site, click your name at the bottom left, and choose "Delete my account". It removes your account and your private messages straight away and frees your username.

Reporting a problem

you can message a moderator. Rule-breaking accounts can be muted, kicked or banned.
